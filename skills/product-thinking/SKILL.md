# Product Thinking

1. Identify what the user actually wants to accomplish.
2. Separate goal from requested UI/technical mechanism.
3. Check unnecessary complexity.
4. Prefer fewer steps and clearer choices.
5. Preserve business constraints.
6. Hide technical details from non-technical users.
7. Challenge clearly inferior implementation details when outcome is clear.
