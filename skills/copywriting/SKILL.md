# Product Copywriting

- lead with value, not implementation,
- make claims specific,
- avoid filler like "powerful", "seamless", "robust", "cutting-edge",
- use concise action-oriented CTAs,
- preserve product terminology,
- keep body copy short unless depth is needed.

Do not let this skill influence unrelated engineering tasks.
